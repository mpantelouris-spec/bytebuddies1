03   Objects
Starter adventure · Collect 3 coins, dodge the lava, find the star.Made for curious minds ✦

Choose a game. Make it yours.

Kart Racing: W / ↑ accelerates, S / ↓ brakes or reverses, A / D steer. Space gives a short turbo boost. Pass the four colored gates in order to complete a lap.

Flight School: Your plane flies forward. W / ↑ climbs, S / ↓ dives, A / D turn. Fly through the golden rings. Follow the altitude hints.

Football: WASD / arrows move your blue player. Get close to the ball and press Space to shoot in the direction you face. Score in the far goal; defend the near goal.

Play: Use WASD or arrow keys to move. Press Space to jump. Collect gold coins, avoid the red lava, and reach the star before time runs out.

Build: Click an object in the world or list. Drag its colored arrows to move it, or type values in the inspector. Add objects from the tray.

Code: Each object has its own blocks. Start with a yellow event, then snap actions inside it. Try changing a coin’s score!

Pause freezes time. Stop brings your original world back. Save project downloads your scene and blocks; Open restores it. Your game and blocks are saved together in the project file.
