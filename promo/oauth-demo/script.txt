This demo is about Google Classroom, and how ByteBuddies connects to it.
Teachers already run their classes in Google Classroom at classroom.google.com.
ByteBuddies asks the teacher to connect that Google Classroom account.
Google shows the ByteBuddies consent screen. The teacher presses Allow.
Courses read-only lets ByteBuddies list the teacher's Google Classroom classes, like Year Three Computing.
Rosters read-only lets ByteBuddies read the People tab — the pupil names on that Google Classroom class.
Profile emails lets ByteBuddies match those Classroom pupils by email to accounts in our platform.
Coursework students lets ByteBuddies post an assignment into Google Classroom classwork, and send a grade back.
We only use Google Classroom data for those teacher features. Pupils keep working in Classroom. Teachers can disconnect at any time.
