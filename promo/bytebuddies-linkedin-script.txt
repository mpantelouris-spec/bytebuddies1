Every child has ideas. ByteBuddies helps turn those ideas into things they can make.

Pupils explore interactive lessons, learn to code, and build games in one primary computing platform.

They solve story-led coding challenges, experimenting, testing, and learning to debug.

They can design their own robots and bring ideas to life in 3D worlds.

Teachers can set work, track progress, and give thoughtful feedback in one place.

From a first line of code to a project of their own, learning becomes active, creative, and visible.

ByteBuddies. Make learning. Make things happen.
