This is ByteBuddies. The whole computing platform, in the browser.

One hundred and fifty-two lessons for Years 3 to 6, mapped to the National Curriculum.

Pupils build games with blocks, costumes and sounds, the way they would in Scratch, only made for class.

They design robots in 3D, then drive them in a simulator. No kits to buy.

When they are ready, they switch to Python in the same place.

Missions, challenges and a teacher classroom sit on top, so you can set work and see who needs help.

Google Classroom ready. Free school pilots for 2026 and 2027.
